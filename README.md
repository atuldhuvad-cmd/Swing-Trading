# Swing Trading for Beginners — Complete India Guide (NSE/BSE)

A step-by-step learning guide with **practical ₹ examples**, **Indian tools**, and **YouTube videos**. Self-contained — no prior context needed.

> **Disclaimer:** Educational only, not SEBI-registered advice. Most beginners lose money. Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose.

---

## What Is Swing Trading?

Swing trading means holding a stock for **2 days to 3 weeks** to capture a short-to-medium price move. You analyze on the **daily chart**, place trades in the **morning or after market close**, and check positions once a day.

| Style | Hold time | Screen time | India product |
|-------|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| Intraday | Same day | High | MIS |
| **Swing** | **2 days–3 weeks** | **Low** | **CNC (delivery)** |
| Investing | Months–years | Very low | CNC |

**Why swing trading suits Indian working professionals:**
- Market is open only **9:15 AM – 3:30 PM IST**
- Analyze after work (4–5 PM or next morning)
- **Zero brokerage** on CNC delivery at most brokers
- No 3:20 PM square-off pressure like intraday

### Step 5: Read candlesticks

```
Bullish candle:          Hammer (buy signal):      Bearish engulfing:
   │                          │                        ┃
   ┃ close > open             ┃                        ┃
   ┃                          ┃ long lower wick        ┃
   │                          │                        │
```

**Learn only these 4 patterns first:**

| Pattern | Signal | Use when |
|---------|--------|----------|
| **Hammer** | Buyers rejected lows | At support in uptrend |
| **Bullish engulfing** | Buyers took control | After pullback |
| **Shooting star** | Sellers rejected highs | At resistance (exit signal) |
| **Doji** | Indecision | Wait — don't trade alone |

- Stock in uptrend, pulls back to ₹3,850
- Day 1: Hammer — low ₹3,820, close ₹3,855
- Day 2: Green candle closes above ₹3,870
- **Signal:** Potential long entry above hammer high

## Phase 3: Three Core Setups with Full ₹ Examples

Use the **MAEE framework**: Market structure → Area of value → Entry trigger → Exits.

---

### Setup 1: Pullback to 50 SMA in Uptrend

**Best beginner setup for India.**

#### Full trade walkthrough — Infosys (hypothetical)

**Step 1 — Market structure**
- Infosys: higher highs, higher lows on daily chart
- Price above 200 SMA at ₹1,420
- Nifty 50 also above 200 SMA ✅

**Step 2 — Area of value**
- 50 SMA at ₹1,480
- Price pulls back from ₹1,560 → ₹1,485

**Step 3 — Entry trigger**
- Hammer candle at ₹1,485 (low ₹1,472, close ₹1,488)
- Next day closes above ₹1,495 → confirmation

**Step 4 — Exits**

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Entry | ₹1,498 |
| Stop loss | ₹1,445 (below swing low) |
| Target | ₹1,590 (prior resistance) |
| Risk/share | ₹53 |
| Reward/share | ₹92 |
| R:R | **1.7:1** (acceptable; aim for 2:1 when possible) |

**Position sizing — ₹1,00,000 account, 1% risk:**

```
Risk amount   = ₹1,00,000 × 1% = ₹1,000
Shares        = ₹1,000 ÷ ₹53 = 18 shares
Position size = 18 × ₹1,498 = ₹26,964
Max loss      = 18 × ₹53 = ₹954 ✅
```

**Zerodha order flow:**
1. Buy 18 shares — product: **CNC**, type: **Limit** at ₹1,498
2. Set **GTT sell** — trigger ₹1,445, limit ₹1,444 (stop-loss)
3. Set **GTT sell** — trigger ₹1,590, limit ₹1,589 (target)

**Timeline:**
- Mon: Hammer forms at 50 SMA
- Tue: Entry triggered at ₹1,498
- Wed–Fri: Price rises to ₹1,560
- Next Mon: Exit at ₹1,590 → **Profit: 18 × ₹92 = ₹1,656 (+1.66% on account)**

**If wrong:** Stop at ₹1,445 → **Loss: ₹954 (−0.95%)**

### Setup 2: Range Trade — Buy at Support

**When:** Stock stuck between support and resistance for 3+ weeks.

#### Full trade walkthrough — ITC (hypothetical)

**Market structure:** Range — support ₹415–₹420, resistance ₹440–₹445 for 5 weeks

**Entry trigger:** Price dips to ₹414 (false break), closes back above ₹418 with bullish engulfing

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Entry | ₹420 |
| Stop loss | ₹412 (below false-break low) |
| Target | ₹442 (just below resistance) |
| Risk/share | ₹8 |
| Reward/share | ₹22 |
| R:R | **2.75:1** ✅ |

**Position sizing — ₹50,000 account, 1% risk:**

```
Risk = ₹500
Shares = ₹500 ÷ ₹8 = 62 shares
Position = 62 × ₹420 = ₹26,040
```

**Key rule:** In a range, take profit **before** resistance. Don't hope for breakout unless you have a separate breakout plan.

## Phase 4: Risk Management (Non-Negotiable)

### Step 7: The 1% rule

**Formula:**

```
Position Size = (Account × Risk %) ÷ (Entry − Stop Loss)
```

#### Three trades compared — ₹1,00,000 account, 1% risk (₹1,000)

| Stock | Entry | Stop | Risk/share | Shares | Position ₹ |
|-------|-------|------|------------|--------|------------|
| TCS | ₹3,800 | ₹3,700 | ₹100 | 10 | ₹38,000 |
| ITC | ₹420 | ₹410 | ₹10 | 100 | ₹42,000 |
| SBI | ₹780 | ₹760 | ₹20 | 50 | ₹39,000 |

Dollar risk stays ~₹1,000 regardless of stock price.

### Step 8: Why you don't need to win every trade

| Win rate | Avg R:R | 10 trades result |
|----------|---------|------------------|
| 40% | 2:1 | 4×₹2,000 − 6×₹1,000 = **+₹2,000** |
| 50% | 2:1 | 5×₹2,000 − 5×₹1,000 = **+₹5,000** |

**Rule:** Minimum **2:1 R:R** on every trade.

---

### Step 9: India-specific risk rules

| Rule | Setting |
|------|---------|
| Risk per trade | **1%** of capital (0.5% while learning) |
| Max open positions | **3–4** |
| Max in one stock | **20%** of capital |
| Weekly loss limit | Stop trading after **−3%** |
| Stock universe | **Nifty 50 / Nifty 100** only |
| Avoid | ASM/GSM stocks, penny stocks, 2%/5% circuit stocks |
| Earnings | Don't hold through quarterly results (unless planned) |

---

### Step 10: Circuit limits — India's biggest extra risk

Stocks have daily **upper/lower circuits** (2%, 5%, 10%, or 20%). If a stock hits lower circuit, **you may not be able to sell**.

**Real scenario:**
- You buy small-cap at ₹200, stop at ₹185
- Bad news overnight → opens at ₹170, hits **10% lower circuit**
- Stop-loss **does not execute**
- You're stuck until circuit opens

**Protection:**
- Trade **Nifty 50** stocks (usually 20% band or no band for F&O stocks)
- Never risk more than 1% per trade
- Understand: **stop-loss is not guaranteed** on gaps or circuits

---

### Step 11: Taxes on swing trades

Swing trades held **under 12 months** = **STCG at 20%** on profit.

| Holding | Tax rate | Notes |
|---------|----------|-------|
| ≤ 12 months (STCG) | **20%** | Most swing trades |
| > 12 months (LTCG) | **12.5%** above ₹1.25L/year exemption | If you hold longer |

**Example:** ₹5,000 gross profit on a 2-week swing trade → ~₹1,000 tax → **₹4,000 net**

Factor tax into your target planning.
